Hand Yaw Math: X–Z Plane Projection

Calculate smooth left-to-right hand rotation (yaw around the vertical Y-axis) by ignoring vertical height and projecting two rigid palm knuckles onto the top-down X–Z plane.

The Geometry (Top-Down View)

Why Anchor to Knuckles Instead of Fingertips?

Fingertips curl, spread, and flex independently—measuring rotation from them introduces massive noise whenever you move your fingers. Instead, anchor to the two outer knuckles that span the rigid width of your palm:

Top-Down Right Triangle Equations
Horizontal Leg
Δx = x17 − x5
Depth Leg
Δz = z17 − z5
Yaw Angle
θ = atan2(Δz, Δx)

When your palm faces straight at the camera, both knuckles sit at the same depth (Δz = 0). Twisting your wrist pushes one knuckle closer and the other farther away, turning the palm width into the hypotenuse of a right triangle solved by atan2(Δz, Δx).

Use worldLandmarks, not landmarks: MediaPipe's worldLandmarks are measured in real-world meters centered at the palm's geometric origin. This eliminates webcam perspective distortion and keeps Δx and Δz on an exact 1:1 physical scale.

03

Three.js Code

Read results.worldLandmarks[0], measure the X–Z vector from Index MCP (5) to Pinky MCP (17), and assign the angle directly to your Three.js mesh.

handYawThree.js 
// worldLandmarks = results.worldLandmarks[0]
const indexMCP = worldLandmarks[5];
const pinkyMCP = worldLandmarks[17];

const dx = pinkyMCP.x - indexMCP.x;
const dz = pinkyMCP.z - indexMCP.z;

// Compute yaw in radians (negate for mirrored selfie webcam)
const yaw = Math.atan2(dz, dx);
mesh.rotation.y = -yaw;
Why LERP is good to have

In a real webcam feed, hand landmarks have slight frame-to-frame jitter, so blending toward the target angle with linear interpolation (e.g., mesh.rotation.y += (targetYaw - mesh.rotation.y) * 0.2) keeps the 3D rotation smooth instead of shaky.

Handedness note: Because your left and right hands mirror each other, the vector from landmark 5 to 17 points in opposite directions on each hand, so swap the subtraction order (or add Math.PI) based on results.handedness if you want both hands to zero out at the same angle.
